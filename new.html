Top Places to Visit

Bodh Gaya

Famous for the Mahabodhi Temple, where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment.

Nalanda

Home to the ancient Nalanda University, one of the world’s first residential universities.

Rajgir

A historic city known for its hot springs and connection to Buddhism & Jainism.

Mahabodhi Temple

The Mahabodhi Temple, located in Bodh Gaya, Bihar, is one of the most important and sacred Buddhist pilgrimage sites in the world.
